Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

gta-clipper ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile info (user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos (video.publish), only for the account that signs in.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the connected Instagram account.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no server and no database. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok, Google or Meta account settings. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored tokens. To ask a question or request deletion, email clipsofhta6@gmail.com.
