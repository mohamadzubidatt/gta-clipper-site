What gta-clipper does

GTA 6 streams run for hours. Most viewers only have a minute. gta-clipper finds the funny, chaotic and impressive moments and turns them into short clips that are easy to watch on a phone.

Finds the moments

Checks new GTA 6 streams and VODs and picks the moments viewers reacted to most.

Makes them vertical

Cuts each moment to a 9:16 clip with readable captions, a title and on-screen credit to the streamer.

A person approves every clip

Nothing is posted automatically. Each clip is watched and approved or rejected by hand.

Publishes everywhere

Approved clips are uploaded to the connected TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts in one step.

How it works

Four steps from a live stream to a posted clip.

Step 1

Fetch

New GTA 6 streams from the followed channels are found by title and downloaded.

Step 2

Cut

Highlights are found from viewer clips and audio peaks, transcribed, and cut into short vertical videos.

Step 3

Review

Every clip waits in a review queue. Clips that are slow, off-topic or not about GTA 6 are rejected.

Step 4

Post

Approved clips are posted through the official TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API and Instagram Graph API.

Features

TikTok sign-in

Connects a TikTok account with Login Kit. The app only asks for basic profile info and permission to post videos.

Creator credit

Every clip names the streamer on screen and in the caption, with a link to the original stream.

Duplicate check

The same moment is never posted twice, even if it appears in several streams or clips.

Upload limits respected

Posts are spaced out to stay inside each platform's daily limits.

Private by design

Sign-in tokens stay on the computer running the app. There is no server and no database.

Takedown on request

Creators can ask for any clip to be removed and it is taken down on every platform.

FAQ

Is anything posted without a person checking it?

No. Every clip is reviewed by hand and only approved clips are posted.

Which streams are clipped?

Only GTA 6 streams. Streams of other games, older GTA titles and roleplay servers are skipped.

I'm a streamer and want a clip removed.

Email clipsofhta6@gmail.com with the link and it will be taken down.

What data does gta-clipper keep?

Only the sign-in tokens needed to post, stored on the app owner's computer. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect my TikTok account?

Remove gta-clipper from the connected apps list in your TikTok security settings. Access stops immediately.
