gta-clipper

gta-clipper is a personal tool that turns GTA streams into short vertical clips and posts them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Only streamers who have given permission are clipped, and every clip credits the streamer and links to the original stream. Every clip is reviewed by hand before it is posted.
